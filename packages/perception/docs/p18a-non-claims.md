# P18A Non-Claims

P18A does not claim that:

- pixel change identifies an object;
- an annotation was detected automatically;
- a changed field caused the recorded action;
- an unchanged field was irrelevant;
- fieldwise mean change captures every perceptually meaningful transition;
- one observation pair is sufficient evidence for a recurring relationship;
- the transition map is a learned world model.

P18A claims only that two exact Source VPMs were compared under one exact field schema and that the resulting measurements and rendered evidence surface are deterministic and content-addressed.
