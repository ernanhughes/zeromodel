# P18A Review Checklist

Review P18A as a deterministic testing instrument, not as an object detector or causal model.

- Confirm the before and after observations are validated against one exact field schema.
- Confirm every field measurement is normalized, deterministic, and included in the artifact identity.
- Confirm the explicit change threshold affects changed counts and artifact identity.
- Confirm annotations bind identities only and cannot alter measured values.
- Confirm the rendered VPM is reproducible and its PNG digest is verified.
- Confirm incompatible schemas and tampered identities are rejected.
- Confirm no wording implies that a changed field caused the action or result.
- Confirm P18B can add expected-transition findings without changing P18A measurements.
